# What this repository is not

- The map is not a diagnostic instrument and not a medical device (PCMS-NOT-001). That is the stated use, not a regulatory opinion.
- It does not replace clinical diagnostic assessment, neuropsychological testing, or accommodation and service eligibility assessments. See [DESIGN-PHILOSOPHY.md](DESIGN-PHILOSOPHY.md).
- It is not the research programme and it is not the computational model. Principles live in the [Cognitive Landscape Research Programme](https://github.com/SynapticFour/cognitive-landscape-research-programme). The library is the [Cognitive Landscape Model](https://github.com/SynapticFour/cognitive-landscape-model).
- It is not an empirical validation. The tier is T0. See [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).
- The ethics statement names what has not happened yet: end-to-end encryption, review under the Declaration of Helsinki, an ethics committee, an ethics advisory board, and an annual review (PCMS-ETHICS-001). That page is a status statement, not an ethics approval. The text is [ethics.md](ethics.md).
- Cloud storage of answers runs only when the person agrees (PCMS-PRIVACY-001). The consent log and the voluntary atlas self-nomination are separate. No questionnaire was submitted on the public site for that claim, so a live write was not observed there.
- A fork or a self-hosted copy is the operator's deployment. Legal documents for that copy are the operator's. See [FORK-AND-HOSTING.md](FORK-AND-HOSTING.md).

Use with young people or in schools needs a local ethical review, consent or assent that fits that setting, and often a separate build.
